# Taxonomy

## Record types

- `brand` — named commercial identity (Štark, Bambi, Knjaz Miloš)
- `product` — specific good (Bananica, Plazma, Negro)
- `company` — legal / historical manufacturer
- `factory` — physical plant
- `location` — city / site of origin or production
- `category`
- `source`
- `image`
- `alias`
- `candidate` — not yet a public product page

## Categories (seed)

Use stable slugs. Serbian public label + English public label.

See repository file for the slug table (slatkisi through ambalaza-dizajn).

A product can have several categories. Primary category is required.

Do not attach a city to a product without a source.

Brand names stay untranslated: Bananica, Smoki, Najlepše želje, Plazma.

Publication states: `draft` · `researching` · `fact_checked` · `published` · `needs_review`
